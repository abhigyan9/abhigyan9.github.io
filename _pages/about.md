Hello! I am Abhigyan Majumdar, a PhD student at the University of California Davis, specializing in System Dynamics & Controls. My work focuses on probabilistic linear modeling of nonlinear systems using Gaussian Process Regression and Koopman Operator theory, along with developing stochastic optimal control schemes to enhance controller performance in robotics, autonomous driving, and vehicle dynamics. I have extensive experience with real-world control systems development and testing, especially in an automotive setting, having led the Propulsion Supervisor Controller development from concept-to-drivable for the Cadillac LYRIQ, as the Propulsion Controls and Modeling Lead of the EcoCAR UC Davis team for years 1 and 2 of the EcoCAR EV Challenge.

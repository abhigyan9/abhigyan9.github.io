Hello! I am Abhigyan Majumdar, a PhD student at the University of California Davis, specializing in System Dynamics & Controls. My work focuses on probabilistic linear modeling of nonlinear systems using Gaussian Process Regression and Koopman Operator theory, along with developing stochastic optimal control schemes to enhance controller performance in robotics, autonomous driving, and vehicle dynamics. Apart from that, I am also leading a electric vehicle thermal modeling and management study to improve drivability and efficiency while ensuring safety of the entire system.

I have extensive experience with real-world control systems development and testing, especially in an automotive setting, having led the Propulsion Controls & Modeling subteam of the [EcoCAR UC Davis team](https://ecocar.ucdavis.edu/) for years 1 and 2 of the [EcoCAR EV Challenge](https://avtcseries.org/about-the-ecocar-ev-challenge/). I led powertrain architecture selection studies and also led the test-driven development of the Propulsion Supervisory Controller from concept-to-drivable for the team vehicle, Nebula, a modified 2023 Cadillac LYRIQ.
